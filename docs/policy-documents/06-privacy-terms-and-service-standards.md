## 1. Privacy policy

### What we hold about you

- Your name, email address, and date of birth
- Your phone number, if you gave us one
- Your accounts, balances and full transaction history
- The support emails you send us and the replies we send back
- Fraud assessments generated when you move money
- For a guardian-supervised account, the link between minor and guardian

### Why we hold it

To operate your account, move money when you ask, screen transactions for fraud, answer your questions, and keep the audit records a bank is expected to keep.

### Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or pass it to third parties for marketing.

Your data is processed by the infrastructure that runs this bank — a database, an email provider, a fraud-scoring service, and an AI assistant that drafts answers to policy questions. Those are processors acting on our instructions, not parties we share your data *with*.

### What the AI assistant sees

When you ask a policy question, the text of your question is sent to a language model to draft an answer. **The model is given our published policy documents and your question. It is not given your balance, your transactions, or anyone else's data.** It cannot look your account up.

### How long we keep it

Account and transaction records are retained permanently, including after an account is closed, because the ledger must stay internally consistent and auditable. Closing an account removes your ability to use it; it does not erase its history.

### Your rights

Email us to ask what we hold about you, or to correct something that's wrong. Requests to erase transaction history cannot be granted for the reason above, and we will tell you that plainly rather than quietly ignoring it.

## 2. Terms and conditions

- Accounts are for personal or business use as opened, and must not be used for anything unlawful.
- You are responsible for the security of the email account your banking is registered to. **Anyone with access to your email can instruct us as you.** Treat it accordingly.
- Instructions are acted on as received. Check the account number before you send — a transfer to the wrong account is not reversible by us (see the Payments policy).
- We may freeze an account where our fraud checks require it, including before contacting you.
- We may decline a loan application without being obliged to explain beyond the reason we give.
- Fees and limits are those published in the Payments policy and may change; the current version of these documents is authoritative.
- This is a demonstration banking system built for a capstone project. It is **not a licensed bank**, holds no real money, and provides no deposit protection.

## 3. What happens automatically, and what needs a person

We deliberately do not send everything to a human. Routing everything to a person makes the answer slower without making it better, and trains people to rubber-stamp.

### Handled automatically, no human step

- Balance and statement enquiries
- Transfers from accounts you hold that pass fraud screening
- Deposits within the published limits
- Loans of Rs 200,000 or less
- Opening a single or joint account
- Scheduled standing order payments
- Producing and sending your account statement
- Paying monthly interest on a savings account
- Withdrawing a loan application of your own that has not been decided yet
- Any policy question we can answer confidently from these documents

### Always involves a person at the bank

- A loan above Rs 200,000
- A policy question our assistant cannot answer confidently from published policy
- Releasing a fraud hold — always, without exception
- Investigating and deciding a dispute
- **Reversing a completed transfer** — there is no automated path to this at all
- Crediting money to a customer account outside of a deposit or a loan (see the Borrowing policy)

In these cases nothing is sent to you until a member of our operations team has reviewed it, and you are told a review is underway rather than being handed a guess.

### Decisions that are yours, not ours

Some approvals are not the bank's to make, and we refer them to you instead:

- A co-holder approving a transfer on an all-signatures joint account
- A parent or guardian approving an account for someone under 18
- Any holder confirming they really want an account closed
- Every holder agreeing before someone is added to, or removed from, a joint account — including the person joining or leaving
- Co-holders giving their side of a dispute raised on a shared account

These arrive as an email carrying a short reference code. You reply with the single word `APPROVE` or `REJECT`.

**We never guess at an unclear answer.** If a reply contains both words, or neither, nothing happens to the request and we write back asking for one clear word. A pending request is never resolved by inference.

## 4. How our assistant answers policy questions

Questions about fees, limits, rules and procedures are answered by an AI assistant that searches these published policy documents and answers **only** from what it finds.

- It is instructed never to invent a policy, a fee or a limit.
- It must cite the documents it used.
- If it cannot find a confident, grounded answer, it does not guess. The question goes to a human specialist and you are told that it has.

If an answer you receive contradicts these documents, these documents are correct — please reply and tell us.

| | |
|---|---|
| Support channel | Email. It is the only channel. |
| Automated replies | Immediate, usually within a minute |
| Human-reviewed replies | Usually within one business day |
| Support hours for human review | Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM PKT |
| Automated banking | 24/7, including weekends and holidays |

You do not need to be a Digital Bank customer to ask a policy question. Anyone can email and ask about fees, limits or how something works. Account-specific information is only ever given to the registered holder.
